I also independently compared all 200 kcal/protein/carbohydrate/fiber values in `usda-selected.json` with the local USDA archive's `food_nutrient.csv`: no mismatches. ## Execution and timing

- **Lentil/chicken bake (Markdown lines 296–319):** already-chopped refrigerated cooked chicken, room-temperature cans and fresh spinach avoid raw-meat prep and a pressure/release tail. About 1.45 kg food in a 13×9-inch shallow dish is plausible at the stated maximum one-inch depth; the explicit split-dish instruction handles excess depth. Covered moisture prevents the precooked breast from roasting dry. Independent elapsed sum is 10–12 preheat/prep + 20–25 heat + 2 finish = 32–39 minutes, consistent with the published 35–40 planning window. Heating time remains uncertain, honestly labeled and overridden by 165°F checks in the center and chicken pieces. No mid-cook intervention is required.
- **Harissa bowls (lines 321–345):** about 1.16 kg chickpeas/cauliflower across two half-sheet pans gives roughly 580 g per pan, a credible single-layer roast. Small fresh florets, patted-dry chickpeas, oil, one combined stir/rack swap, cold lemon yogurt and final hemp give a defensible flavor/texture mechanism. Independent ordinary-start sum is 10–12 + 22–25 + 2 = 34–39 minutes; 30–40 is a planning range, not a hard promise. Sauce preparation occurs during roasting. One intervention and a 12-minute estimated active budget are plausible with pre-cut florets and bagged greens.
- **Chicken/potatoes (lines 347–371):** the microwave is genuinely part of the method, with wattage, piece size, water, vented cover, drain and fork-tender endpoint. Its 6–8 passive minutes can overlap chicken/bean prep. The 12–16 ready/load estimate + 20–25 roast + 2 finish = 34–43 minutes fits the explicit 35–45 exception. The vegetable pan's separate halves hold approximately 567 g potato pieces and 454 g beans: a plausible but relatively tight layout on a full 18×13 pan, not a small pan. The single-layer instruction and smaller-pan timing warning are material. One combined bean addition/rack swap is required. Fifteen active minutes is the tightest non-keto budget, but cut baby potatoes, trimmed beans and unfolded thighs keep it defensible as an estimate.
- **Gnocchi (lines 373–398):** initial load is about 604 g/pan; final load after adding drained beans is about 844 g/pan. Two full half-sheet pans are plausible for the measured gnocchi, broccoli, tomatoes and beans. Beans enter later, reducing drying; oil and tomato moisture support gnocchi interiors while spacing/stirring promote exterior roasting. One combined addition/stir/rack swap is explicit. Independent sum is 10–12 + (12 + 13–18) + 2 = 37–44 minutes, consistent with 40–45. I opened [DeLallo's manufacturer recipe](https://www.delallo.com/recipe/sheet-pan-pesto-gnocchi/), which indeed roasts packaged gnocchi without boiling and includes legumes. Its different vegetables/sauce and 400°F/30-minute method support the mechanism, not an exact timing transfer.

No food quantity is stranded or consumed twice in these methods. No initial saute, dry-bean cooking, overnight prerequisite, reduction or continuous stirring remains. The bake and potato dinner use poultry; the common clean-equipment and handling instructions apply. [FoodSafety.gov's current temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) supports the 165°F poultry/reheat endpoint. Foil covering, small pieces and fork-tender checks do not replace the thermometer.
